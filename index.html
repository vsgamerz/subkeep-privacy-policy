SubKeep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

SubKeep is published by VS Developers. This policy covers the Android app SubKeep.

Data stored on your device

Subscription names, amounts, billing dates, categories, notes, and reminder settings stay on your device. SubKeep does not require an account and does not connect to your bank.

Ads

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may collect device and advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads, including through the Google consent form where required. See Google’s privacy policy.

Analytics and crashes

The app uses Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to understand usage and fix crashes. This can include app events, device type, and crash logs. See Firebase privacy information.

What we do not do

We do not sell your subscription list. We do not ask you to sign in. There are no in-app purchases in the current version.
